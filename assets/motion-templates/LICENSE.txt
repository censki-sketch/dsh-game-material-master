The bundled .rgba.gz files preserve the original sprite poses, colors, alpha,
and eight compass directions as compressed raw pixels. Runtime previews convert
them to neutral grayscale. Code licensing and generated output rights are separate.
The importer is scripts/import-motion-templates.py (development-only Pillow).
